Threat Summary On September 27th 2026, Citrix disclosed multiple vulnerabilities affecting NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway. Arctic Wolf tracking indicates that CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772 have been exploited in attacks against NetScaler devices as zero-days. Additional CVEs are also disclosed in the Citrix Security Bulletin.
